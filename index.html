      History

        Click a step to go back to it. Later steps stay in the list until you change something.

        Sizes

        The decals are drawn once on this size and moved onto the others along each tube, then unwrapped for each size.

        Film

        Stretch colours, warnings and relief cuts use this limit. The presets are starting points; check your film's data sheet.

        Sheet

        Cut lines are black paths in millimetres on a "cut" layer. Labels and the sheet outline sit on their own layers so you can hide them in the cutter software.

        Projects and templates

        Kept in this browser on this computer. Download a project or your templates to back them up or move them to another computer.

          A starting point is a frame with the decals you always use on it (for example one bike model). New from it opens a copy as a new, unsaved project, so the starting point itself never changes.

            Save a decal as a template with Save to library in the Decal panel (Edit mode). Placing a template works like placing artwork: move over the model, click, adjust, Apply.

              Check the model size

              Model files often do not say which unit they use. Pick the one that gives the real size; decals, stretch and the cut sheet are all measured from it.

              Place SVG artwork

              Each closed shape becomes an editable decal. Set the real size, then move over the model to see it and click to preview the placement before applying it.

              Frame from geometry

              Pick a preset and size, or type the numbers from a geometry chart. Tubes are built from your diameters and tube shape (round, carbon oval or aero), so use this for layout and sizing. Production patterns still need the real frame model.

              Tube shape and diameters
              Paste a geometry chart

              Copy the geometry table from a brand website and paste it here. Rows like stack, reach, head and seat angles, head tube, seat tube, top tube slope, chainstay, BB drop and wheelbase are recognised, with one column per size.
