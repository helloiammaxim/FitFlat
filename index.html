              FitFlat

              Draw in 3D. Export flat. Done.
              1. This is your frameA start frame is loaded so you can try things right away. Drag the background to turn it, right-drag to slide it, scroll to zoom.
              2. Pick your frameFrame from geometry has ready frames for road, gravel and mountain bikes in several sizes. Or paste the geometry chart from a brand's website and it builds that frame.
              3. Or load your own 3D modelGot a 3D scan or a CAD file of the frame? Open loads .glb, .obj and .stl models. Decals drawn on the real shape fit the real tubes.
              4. Draw a decalWith Draw, click points around the area you want to protect and click the first point again to close it. Ready shapes like a rectangle or a pill work too.
              5. Make it fitEdit drags points and moves the whole decal. Mirror puts a copy on the other side of the frame. The Knife splits a decal in two.
              6. Decals: your piecesEvery decal you draw lands in this list. Rename, duplicate, lock or delete it here, or combine two with Union, Subtract and Intersect. History underneath steps back through your changes.
              7. View: how you look at itJump to a fixed side, show a grid or a reference photo, and save views you need again. Stretch colours each decal by how hard the film has to stretch; red means too far.
              8. Sheet: ready to cutAll pieces are laid out flat here. Pick your film and sheet size, then download the SVG or DXF for your cutter.
              9. Need more?This button plays the intro again. The manual has every tool and shortcut.
              Manual

              Where should it open?

              goes all the way round the tube. Film can't lie flat as a closed ring, so the piece gets one seam, a cut where its two edges meet on the frame. Pick a side where it won't show; the dashed line marks it.

              Keep it editable?

              Editable keeps the original decals inside the result: select the combined piece, pick one of them in the list and change it, and the result follows. Apply turns the result into one ordinary outline.

              Check the model size

              Model files often do not say which unit they use. Pick the one that gives the real size; decals, stretch and the cut sheet are all measured from it.

              Place SVG artwork

              Each closed shape becomes an editable decal. Set the real size, then move over the model to see it and click to preview the placement before applying it.

              Frame from geometry

              Pick a preset and size, or type the numbers from a geometry chart. Tubes are built from your diameters and tube shape (round, carbon oval or aero), so use this for layout and sizing. Production patterns still need the real frame model.

              Tube shape and diameters
              Paste a geometry chart

              Copy the geometry table from a brand website and paste it here. Rows like stack, reach, head and seat angles, head tube, seat tube, top tube slope, chainstay, BB drop and wheelbase are recognised, with one column per size.
