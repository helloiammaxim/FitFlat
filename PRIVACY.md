# Privacy

**Your work stays with you.** FitFlat collects no personal data and sends nothing about your projects anywhere.

## Your projects
Models, scans, drawings, projects and exports stay on your computer: in your browser or in the folder you choose. There is no account, no cloud, no tracking, no analytics, no ads and no cookies.

## The website fitflat.app
- It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA, a Microsoft company). Like for any website, its servers see your IP address when you open a page; that is needed to deliver it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Downloads of the desktop app come from GitHub too.
- To sign in, your licence file is read in your browser only and never uploaded. "Remember on this computer" keeps a key in your browser's local storage (no cookie; you can delete it any time in your browser's site settings).

## The desktop app
- It goes online only to check for updates and for withdrawn licences (fitflat.app and GitHub). No project or personal information is sent. You can switch this off: Settings → Licence → "Check fitflat.app for updates…". Then download new versions here yourself.
- The machine code is a one-way code made from your computer's ID. It leaves your computer only when you send it to us for a licence.
- Office seats: the licence host in your office sees the user name and machine code of the computers using a seat, inside your network only.
- A free trial is recorded on your computer only.

## Licences and contact
When you buy a licence, we keep your company name, contact email, licence number and machine codes to issue and support it (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), for as long as the licence runs and the law requires us to keep business records. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and move your data and to object, and you can complain to a data protection authority.
