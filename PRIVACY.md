# Privacy

**Your work stays with you.** FitFlat collects no personal data and sends nothing about your projects anywhere.

## Your projects
Models, scans, drawings, projects and exports stay on your computer: in your browser or in the folder you choose. There is no account, no cloud, no tracking, no analytics, no ads and no cookies.

## The website fitflat.app
- It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA, a Microsoft company). Like for any website, its servers see your IP address when you open a page; that is needed to deliver it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Downloads of the desktop app come from GitHub too.
- To sign in, your licence file is read in your browser only and never uploaded. "Remember on this computer" keeps a key in your browser's local storage (no cookie; you can delete it any time in your browser's site settings).

## The desktop app
- It goes online only to check for updates and for withdrawn licences (fitflat.app and GitHub). No project or personal information is sent. You can switch this off: Settings → Licence → "Check fitflat.app for updates…". Then download new versions here yourself.
- The machine code is a one-way code made from your computer's ID. It leaves your computer only when you send it to us for a licence.
- Office seats: the licence host in your office sees the user name and machine code of the computers using a seat, inside your network only.
- A free trial is recorded on your computer only.

## Questions and requests
Use **Send a request** (in FitFlat under Help, or on fitflat.app): we answer by email, and delete the request 12 months after it is closed.

## Licences
When you buy a licence, we keep your company name, contact email, licence number and machine codes to issue and support it (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), for as long as the licence runs and the law requires us to keep business records. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and move your data and to object, and you can complain to a data protection authority.

### 2. Your projects

FitFlat works on your computer. Models, scans, drawings, decals, projects, templates and exports are processed only on your device (in your browser or in a folder you choose) and are never sent to us or anyone else. We have no access to them.

### 3. The website fitflat.app

**Hosting.** The website, the web app and the downloads are hosted by GitHub Pages and GitHub Releases (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page or download a file, GitHub's servers process your IP address, date and time, the requested address, the referring page and your browser's identification, to deliver the content and keep the service secure. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in delivering the website securely). GitHub keeps these logs according to its own [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement); we do not receive them.

**No cookies, no tracking.** We use no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no fonts or scripts from other servers.

**Signing in.** Your licence file is read only in your browser and is not uploaded. If you tick "Remember on this computer", a key derived from it is stored in your browser's local storage, which you asked for and which is strictly necessary for that function (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can remove it at any time in your browser's site settings.

### 4. The desktop app

**Updates and withdrawn licences.** The app asks fitflat.app and GitHub whether a new version exists and fetches the list of withdrawn licences. These requests carry your IP address and the app version, nothing about you or your projects. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest, and yours, in a secure, current app; it also serves our duties to provide security updates). You can switch this off under Settings → Licence; you then download new versions from the website yourself.

**Machine code.** The machine code is a one-way code calculated on your computer from its identifier; it does not reveal the identifier. It leaves your computer only if you send it to us to get a licence.

**Office seats.** If your company shares licence seats on its network, the computer that shares them sees the user name and machine code of the computers using a seat. This stays within your network; we receive nothing.

**Trial.** The start of a free trial is recorded only on your computer.

### 5. Licences and customers

When you buy or ask for a licence, we process your name or company, contact email, billing details, the licence number and type, the machine codes you send us and the term of the licence, to issue, deliver and support the licence and to invoice it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract and steps before it) and Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (tax and commercial record-keeping).

We keep these data for as long as the licence runs and support may be needed, and invoices and business letters for as long as the law requires (usually 6 or 10 years, § 257 HGB, § 147 AO). Licence records are kept on our own computer with backups in Microsoft OneDrive (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd.) and a private GitHub repository (GitHub, Inc.).

### 6. Requests and support

If you use **Send a request** (in the app or on the website), we process the details you enter: name, email address, your message, and if you add them, your licence number, the app version and your operating system. The message is passed through a small relay at Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) and stored as a ticket in a private GitHub repository that only we can read; Cloudflare does not keep it, and your IP address is not stored with the ticket. We answer by email (Gmail, Google Ireland Ltd.). If you write to us by email directly, we process your email the same way.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request is about a licence or a purchase, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering you). We delete a ticket 12 months after it is closed, unless we must keep it longer (for example as a business letter, see section 5).

### 8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Art. 16), deleted (Art. 17) or its processing restricted (Art. 18), and to receive it in a common format (Art. 20). Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f), you can **object** at any time for reasons arising from your particular situation (Art. 21). Where you gave consent, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). To use these rights, write to us (section 1).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state where you live or work.

### 9. Other points

Providing your data is voluntary. Without the details needed for a licence we cannot issue one, and without an email address we cannot answer a request. We make no automated decisions and do no profiling. Connections to fitflat.app, GitHub and the request relay are encrypted (TLS); licences are signed so they cannot be forged.
